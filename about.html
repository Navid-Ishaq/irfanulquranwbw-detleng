About Irfan-ul-Quran

Understanding the Holy Qur'an, Verse by Verse

Irfan-ul-Quran Verse-by-Verse Learning is a digital educational initiative by DeTLeng, designed to help learners understand, remember, and practise complete Qur'anic Ayat through Urdu and English translations.

This learning platform is based on Irfan-ul-Quran, the Qur'anic translation work of Shaykh-ul-Islam Professor Dr. Muhammad Tahir-ul-Qadri.

Through interactive flashcards, multiple-choice questions, and structured learning activities, the platform aims to make verse-by-verse learning more accessible, organised, and engaging.

About Shaykh-ul-Islam Professor Dr. Muhammad Tahir-ul-Qadri

Professor Dr. Muhammad Tahir-ul-Qadri is an internationally recognised Islamic scholar, author, researcher, and educator. He was born on 19 February 1951 in Jhang, Pakistan.

He pursued both traditional Islamic scholarship and modern academic education. He studied Islamic sciences under distinguished scholars and later completed advanced university studies in Islamic Studies and Law, including a doctorate in Islamic Law.

He served as a lecturer and professor at the University of the Punjab, where he contributed to Islamic legal education and research.

Dr. Tahir-ul-Qadri is the founder of Minhaj-ul-Quran International, an organisation devoted to Islamic education, spiritual development, interfaith understanding, peace, and humanitarian welfare.

His extensive scholarly contributions cover Qur'anic studies, Hadith, Islamic jurisprudence, spirituality, Seerah, education, and contemporary Islamic thought.

His publications and lectures have reached audiences across the world, contributing to a greater understanding of Islamic teachings.

Among his important scholarly works is Irfan-ul-Quran, which makes the meanings and message of the Holy Qur'an accessible to readers in Urdu and English.

About Irfan-ul-Quran

عرفان القرآن — Irfan-ul-Quran

Irfan-ul-Quran is a translation of the Holy Qur'an by Professor Dr. Muhammad Tahir-ul-Qadri.

It aims to communicate Qur'anic meanings in clear, expressive, and accessible language while reflecting the spiritual, linguistic, and contextual dimensions of the original Arabic text.

The Urdu and English translations help readers explore the message of the Qur'an and develop a deeper understanding of its teachings.

Irfan-ul-Quran serves as an educational resource for students, teachers, researchers, and general readers interested in understanding the Holy Qur'an.

Urdu and English Translations

The DeTLeng Verse-by-Verse platform uses Urdu and English translation text from Irfan-ul-Quran as its reference material.

Each complete Arabic Ayat is presented with its corresponding Urdu and/or English translation according to the learner’s selected mode.

The aim is to support understanding of complete Qur’anic verses, meaningful revision, and independent learning.

These learning activities support understanding and recall. They do not replace the original Arabic text, detailed Qur’anic commentary, or qualified scholarly guidance.

Irfan-ul-Quran Verse-by-Verse Learning by DeTLeng

Learn. Understand. Remember.

This platform transforms complete Qur’anic verses and their translations into an interactive learning experience.

Structured Qur'anic Learning

The Holy Qur'an is organised into 30 Paras (Juz). Each Para is divided into four learning sections according to the selected Qur'anic quarter boundaries, creating 120 manageable study sets.

Verse-by-Verse Understanding

Learn complete Arabic Ayat with their Urdu and English meanings and corresponding Qur’anic references.

Interactive Flashcards

Practise recognising complete Arabic verses and recalling their meanings through interactive flashcards.

Multiple-Choice Practice

Strengthen learning through questions that encourage active recall and reinforce vocabulary knowledge.

Learning and Test Modes

Use Learning Mode for guided practice and instant feedback, or Test Mode to assess your understanding independently.

Qur'anic References

Each verse retains its Para, Surah, and Ayat reference, helping learners locate the passage within the Holy Qur’an.

Our Educational Vision

Our vision is to make Qur’anic Verse-by-Verse learning accessible to students of different ages and educational backgrounds.

By combining traditional Qur'anic translation resources with modern digital learning techniques, DeTLeng seeks to encourage consistent study, meaningful understanding, and lifelong engagement with the Holy Qur'an.
